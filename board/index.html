Picking a post off the board

181 finished frames are sitting in Figma. The making is done, so the time now goes on choosing, and this page gets that down to about a minute.

What is on the board

COUNTED 31 AUGUST

41 designs, each drawn in four sizes. The sizes are the feed post (1080×1350), the square (1080×1080), the story (1080×1920) and the link preview (1200×628). You never build a size. Pick the design, then take the size you need from the row underneath it.

Two sets break that pattern on purpose. The Carousel comes in three sizes because a link preview cannot swipe. The Reels set is four different frames rather than four sizes: cover, hook, step, end card, in the order a reel needs them.

Every section name is a link. It opens Figma at that section, so you land on the right part of the board instead of hunting across it.

Choose by the reason, not by the picture

Scrolling the board until something looks nice takes twenty minutes and lands on the same three templates. Start from the sentence instead, and finish it out loud:

“I am posting today because…”

  • …this product needs to move. Products Advertisement. Box Advertisement if it is a basket.
  • …someone asked me what to do with it. Recipes, or Utility & How-to.
  • …something happens on a date. Event, or Campaign & Seasonal.
  • …I want people to see there are humans here. Community.
  • …I have one fact worth knowing. Stat & Facts, or Nutritionist Quote.
  • …I have nothing and I still want to post. Community, and take the Supplier story. You have twenty of these frames for that reason.

If no sentence fits, today has no post in it. A quiet Tuesday costs less than a post whose point nobody can name.

Two looks, and when to take which

Inside eight of the fourteen sections the frames split into two columns. The left ones have round corners and clean edges. The right ones have torn paper and peeling pages, and a line above the column says CRAFT.

  • Round is for an ordinary week. A product, a recipe, opening hours.
  • Torn is for an occasion. A festival, a launch, a basket, the end of a season.

The occasion decides it, not the section. A product post can be torn when that product is the new thing this month, and a campaign post can be round when it is the third one in a row. Torn every week stops meaning anything by the fourth.

Using a frame without breaking it

  1. Duplicate the frame you picked. Work on the copy, always.1 min
  2. Drag your photo onto the photo shape. It crops itself to fit.1 min
  3. Click each line of text and type over it. Move nothing.4 min
  4. Check that nothing sits in the red bands top and bottom. Instagram puts its own buttons there.1 min
  5. Export at 1x, PNG. The frame name becomes the file name.1 min

Never press Detach instance. Buttons, badges and the page dots are shared parts. Detach one and it stops following the theme, so the next time the colours change that post keeps the old ones and nothing tells you why.

If a line runs off the edge, your text is longer than the slot. Cut words instead of shrinking the type. The type sizes match across all 181 frames on purpose, and one post in a smaller size shows up in the grid.

If you take one thing from this page

Finish the sentence before you open Figma. I am posting today because… By the end of that sentence the section is chosen, and the choosing is over.
