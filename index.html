The website

Nobody asked for this review. Kir ran it because the shop is where the money comes from, and because the design system only pays off if the site actually uses it. Here is what he found, what he already built, and what it would take to land it.

Of the 623 KB of fonts, 546 KB draws six words. The nine icons cost 299 KB because they arrive as whole icon fonts; the same nine as small files weigh 8 KB.

Roughly eight hundred kilobytes leaves the page. Google measures loading speed and uses it when ranking shops, and a phone on a weak signal feels the difference first.

Two things that cost nothing today and could cost later

  • The keyboard outline is switched off across the whole site. Somebody deleted it on purpose, two lines of code. Anyone who browses without a mouse - and that includes people using screen readers - cannot see where they are on the page. Since June 2025 the European Accessibility Act applies to online shops selling in the EU. Kir is not a lawyer and this page is not legal advice, but it is worth putting in front of yours.
  • Every page heading is hidden. One line of code hides the main title on all pages. Google reads that heading to understand what a page is about, so the shop is handing it nothing.

Four questions for the printer

BLOCKING

Could you put these to them, or tell Kir who to ask? The first one is the only thing standing between the catalogue and a print-ready file.

  • Bleed: 3 mm or 5 mm on all four sides? This sets the sheet size, so the print file cannot be built until the number is known.
  • CMYK profile and paper. Which colour profile, coated or offset stock, and what weight? The colours are in screen colour today and have to be converted.
  • Crop marks. Do they add them when they impose the sheets, or do they expect them in the file? Kir assumes the first.
  • Small text in one ink. Please confirm that text below 14 pt prints in black only. At 9 pt a four-colour black gives a coloured fringe around every letter as soon as the press shifts.

One request on top of the four: a proof of the cover on the real paper before the run. The cover is one large flat blue with white text, the biggest area of solid colour in the book, so any colour shift shows there first.

Text corrections, and the missing intro

YOUR CALL

Eight corrections in the basket pages. They come from the old file and were left untouched so you can confirm them, because some may be deliberate: proboóticas → probióticas, eligir → elegir, frtias → fritas, Sangía → Sangría, Castell D'Aixa → Castell d'Aixa, L' Amo → L'Amo, "Nuestra recomendación:" without the colon, and prices always written as 25 € with a space. Say yes to all and they go in one pass.

The baskets have no intro text. The eleven basket pages open with no paragraph, and the old catalogue has none either, so there is nothing to copy and writing it here would mean inventing your voice. Two ways: one short paragraph of about thirty words used on all eleven, or one of about twenty words per basket. The second reads better - a shared paragraph on your most expensive product looks like filler - but it is eleven texts.
