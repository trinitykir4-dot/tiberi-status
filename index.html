Four questions for the printer

BLOCKING

Could you put these to them, or tell Kir who to ask? The first one is the only thing standing between the catalogue and a print-ready file.

  • Bleed: 3 mm or 5 mm on all four sides? This sets the sheet size, so the print file cannot be built until the number is known.
  • CMYK profile and paper. Which colour profile, coated or offset stock, and what weight? The colours are in screen colour today and have to be converted.
  • Crop marks. Do they add them when they impose the sheets, or do they expect them in the file? Kir assumes the first.
  • Small text in one ink. Please confirm that text below 14 pt prints in black only. At 9 pt a four-colour black gives a coloured fringe around every letter as soon as the press shifts.

One request on top of the four: a proof of the cover on the real paper before the run. The cover is one large flat blue with white text, the biggest area of solid colour in the book, so any colour shift shows there first.
